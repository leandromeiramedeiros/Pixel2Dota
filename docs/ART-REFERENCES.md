# Hero sprite style: director references (look only, never commit)

Images are in `art-refs/` (gitignored): portrait-bust.webp, lineup-invoker-dazzle-rubick-sf.png, lineup-spirits.png, windranger.png,
portraits-poses.webp and death-prophet.png. Huskar, Arc Warden, Visage and Ursa were shown in chat but not saved; the notes below record them.

## Common rules drawn from all of them
- Hand-pixeled clusters. No 3D-render smoothness, no noise, no blobs.
- Heroic proportions (about 5–6 heads). A strong, asymmetric, grounded pose that is readable as a silhouette.
- A solid near-black outline on the silhouette; dark coloured lines separate interior forms.
- Big flat planes of 2–4 tones per material, with hue-shifted ramps (cool shadows, warm highlights).
- Contrast: a dark interior against bright edges (rim) in a contrasting hue.
- Glow: a bright core, hard-edged angular shapes and sparse sparkle or ember pixels. No soft blur blobs.
- Cloth and wings: big pointed panels with 3–4 tones and hard outlines.
- Limited palette per hero (Visage: muted bronze plus teal-grey, with only the accents glowing cyan).

## Per hero
- **Huskar:** a wide lunge, a banded red-orange flame streamer from the helmet and teal skin. A model for Lina's hair.
- **Arc Warden:** angular shard shapes, a cold hard-edged backlight against a warm dark interior, and magenta sparkles.
- **Visage:** a muted bronze and teal palette, translucent wings in flat tones, and glow kept only to the eyes and runes.
- **Ursa (skin):** a squat, wide, symmetric front-facing stance with arms out and claws raised. Weapons get a thin cyan rim; the motion trails are hot-pink slashes with scattered pink pixels. The palette is muted (slate, maroon, leather) with one hot accent.
- **Rubick (lineup-invoker-dazzle-rubick-sf.png):** a silver-white mask with green eyes, green and gold robe, and a tall thin build.
- **Pudge (portraits-poses.webp):** a fat, friendly cartoon build, green mohawk, apron and hook.
- **Invoker (lineup + portraits-poses):** blonde, collar, three coloured orbs with sparkle.

## Portraits (director, verbatim: "PORTRAITS ARE ONLY HEAD. TAKE INSPIRATION FROM THE ANGLE AND POSE FOR THE PORTRAITS")
- Head only, with a sliver of neck or collar at most. No shoulders or bust.
- The angle and pose come from the references: a 3/4 view, the head tilted or turned with attitude, never a flat front-on mugshot.
- Same rules as the body: solid dark outline, flat hue-shifted planes, glowing eyes or accents where the hero has them.
- The HUD portrait box is 32x42 art px (tall); fill it with the head.
